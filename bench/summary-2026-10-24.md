prefill), llama-server build `b11868-78a8ae14b`, RTX 4070 Ti SUPER 16 GB (16,376
MiB). Decode = median of 3 after one discarded warm-up request; cold prefill = median
of 3 with `cache_prompt` false and no warm-up (cold by design). Every server was
